Welcome

Six rooms, six beloved authors, one classic Portland house

In the Division / Clinton neighbourhood of Southeast Portland - a walkable hotspot with a nationally renowned restaurant scene - the Bluebird blends the warmth of a classic Portland inn with modern amenities: fast Wi-Fi, luxury mattresses and endless hot water. Every room has a private bathroom and is uniquely styled, and the big social rooms, guest kitchen and garden invite you to make the house your own.

  • Complimentary continental breakfast
  • Fast Wi-Fi throughout
  • Guest kitchen - cook, snack, make tea
  • Private bathroom in every room
  • Garden & picnic area
  • Laundry machines included
  • Large social rooms & fireplace room
  • Free street parking
