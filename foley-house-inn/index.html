Welcome

The Foley House Inn is a 19-guestroom bed and breakfast made up of two lavishly renovated 1896 mansions facing Chippewa Square, at 14 West Hull Street, Savannah. Rooms feature private baths, luxurious linens and period furniture; every stay includes a full Southern breakfast and a complimentary glass of wine on arrival. Begin the day with coffee in the parlor or the garden, and let the concierge team help plan your Savannah day.
