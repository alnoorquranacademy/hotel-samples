Welcome to Barbican House

Barbican House is a Victorian four-diamond guest house in York, overlooking the city’s famous medieval walls. This 8-room bed & breakfast is known for its individually decorated rooms — no two alike — and a location that puts the whole of historic York within a stroll. Guests rate us 4.6 on Google.
