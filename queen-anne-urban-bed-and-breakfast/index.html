About Queen Anne Urban Bed & Breakfast

Queen Anne Urban Bed & Breakfast pairs Victorian charm with a genuinely green ethos: an eco, zero-waste oasis across two historic houses at 2147 Tremont Pl. Expect artist-touched rooms - one even has a hand-painted aspen ceiling - a community dining table and a garden made for slow mornings.
