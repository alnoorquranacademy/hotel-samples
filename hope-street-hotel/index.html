Welcome to Hope Street

An independent, individual boutique hotel on Liverpool's most famous street, between the city's two cathedrals in the Georgian Quarter. Converted warehouse style, a spa-led approach to relaxation and 149 rooms — each one designed to feel personal, calm and quietly luxurious.

Hope Street & the Georgian Quarter

Hope Street links Liverpool's two cathedrals — the Anglican Cathedral at one end and the Metropolitan Cathedral at the other — with the Philharmonic Hall along the way. From the hotel you are in the heart of the Georgian Quarter, surrounded by culture, dining and the city's creative life.
