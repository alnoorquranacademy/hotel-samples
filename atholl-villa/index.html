Welcome

A Warm Welcome Awaits

Atholl Villa is a 4 Star bed and breakfast enjoying a superb location in the pretty town of Pitlochry, set in spacious, tree-bordered grounds at the quiet southern end of Atholl Road.

Homely, comfortable, well appointed

Our ten en-suite bedrooms are bright, tastefully decorated and appointed to a high standard, across the ground, first and second floors — with quality bedding, Freeview LED televisions, refreshment trays and daily housekeeping. A few bedrooms still retain their feature Victorian fireplaces.

In the morning, relax in our large conservatory overlooking the garden and choose from a selection of freshly prepared quality breakfasts — including our traditional Scottish breakfast, Buckie smoked haddock and Scottish smoked salmon.

One of our ground floor bedrooms is an enabled facility with level access for guests with restricted mobility, and our guest lounge offers comfort and tranquillity at the end of an active day.
