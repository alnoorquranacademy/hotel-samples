Welcome

Pueblo Bonito Inn, at 138 West Manhattan Avenue, has welcomed Santa Fe travellers for over 30 years. The inn is housed in an original 1800s adobe estate, thoughtfully restored, with handcrafted New Mexican furniture, exposed adobe walls and wood-beamed ceilings. Guests enjoy complimentary tea, coffee and snacks, free WiFi and on-site parking — and the best rate, always, when booking direct.

Rooms & Rates

King Guestroom

Our most spacious room, with a plush king bed, handcrafted New Mexican furniture, exposed adobe or wood-beamed ceilings, and in many rooms a traditional kiva fireplace.

Queen Guestroom
