Welcome

Location & Exploring Canterbury

House Of Agnes sits within Canterbury's historic streets, a short stroll from Canterbury Cathedral and the Christchurch Gate. Explore the medieval lanes, the River Stour and the city's independent shops and restaurants, then return to the calm of our garden.
