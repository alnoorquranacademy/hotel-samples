Welcome

A home from home in the Lakes

Denehurst is a traditional Lakeland stone guest house on Queens Drive, a quiet residential street between Windermere and Bowness.

The house has six en-suite double bedrooms, all individual in style and character — including superior rooms and a romantic four-poster. Your hosts have been welcoming guests here for nearly 20 years, and pride themselves on the details.

Breakfast is, of course, included in the room rate — the perfect start before a day on the lake or the fells.
