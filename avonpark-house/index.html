Location & the area

Avonpark House sits in Stratford-upon-Avon, the Warwickshire market town on the River Avon where William Shakespeare was born. Stroll the riverside, visit Shakespeare’s Birthplace on Henley Street, see Holy Trinity Church across the water, and enjoy the town’s theatres, shops and cafés — all within easy reach of the house.
