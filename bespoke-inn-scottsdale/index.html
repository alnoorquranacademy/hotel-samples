Welcome

A Courtyard Hideaway, Purpose-Built in 2013

With just four suites and six guest rooms gathered around a courtyard and a 43-foot infinity-edge pool, Bespoke Inn offers an intimate, design-led stay in Old Town Scottsdale. Dine at Virtù Honest Craft on site, borrow a complimentary Pashley bicycle, and bring your dog — we are proudly pet friendly.
