Welcome

A home from home, run by Susan & Andrew

Carena House is a traditional Victorian bed and breakfast in Canterbury, personally run by Susan and Andrew.

There are five en-suite rooms — singles, doubles and a twin — each simply and comfortably furnished in keeping with the house. It is a personal, home-stay kind of place: unhurried, friendly and quietly proud of its welcome.

You will find us at the green sign on the London Road side of the city — parking is at the rear.
