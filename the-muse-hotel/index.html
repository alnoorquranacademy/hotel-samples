Welcome

A mid-century desert hideaway, designed around the pool

Tucked into a quiet Palm Springs courtyard, The Muse is an adults-only boutique hotel where every suite opens onto the heated pool. Each of the nine suites is named for a legendary muse - Sophia, Edie, Bowie, Kate, Barbie, Brigitte, Audrey, Marilyn and Taylor - and styled in playful retro mid-century colour. Come for a girls trip, a bachelorette weekend, or book the whole hotel and make it yours.

  • Heated courtyard pool & six-person hot tub
  • Adults only (15+)
  • Pet friendly - dogs welcome
  • Full kitchens in most suites
  • Complimentary grab-and-go breakfast
  • Free parking & high-speed WiFi
  • Private patios; four suites with private yards
  • Concierge by phone, text or email, daily
