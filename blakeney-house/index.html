Welcome to Blakeney House

Restaurant • Bar • Rooms — a 5-star AA rated Victorian house tucked away in the heart of Blakeney village.

Our 2 AA Rosette restaurant often gets the limelight, but our 10 boutique bedrooms are just as much the star of the show. Each is named after a favourite local place in Norfolk, and each has its own unique style, layout and character.

Residents enjoy a complimentary breakfast each morning, a flute of our signature sparkling Italian rosé during their stay, access to our hidden cocktail bar and lounge, and priority booking in the restaurant.

Blakeney & the North Norfolk Coast

Hollyhock-lined streets, a boat-lined quay and the famous Blakeney Point seals.

There is a magic in Blakeney you don't get elsewhere. In summer the village buzzes — independent shops, cafés and delis, paddle boards to rent and seal trips to book from the quay. Out of season, the coast path empties and the salt marshes are all yours.

Holkham, Wells-next-the-Sea, Cley and Morston are all close by, and the North Norfolk Coast Path runs right past the village.
