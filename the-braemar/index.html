Welcome to The Braemar

Set in the heart of Old Shanklin, a short stroll from the beach, the Old Village and Shanklin Chine.

All our 9 bedrooms offer en-suite facilities and are tastefully decorated to provide a cosy, relaxing retreat after a busy day exploring the island. Choose from single, double, twin, triple and family rooms — and we can accommodate a ground-floor room if requested when booking.

Every room has a colour TV, free Wi-Fi, a hairdryer and a well-stocked hospitality tray. We are pet friendly, so your dog is welcome to holiday too.

Old Shanklin & the Isle of Wight

Thatched cottages, a famous chine, and golden sand minutes from the door.

The Braemar sits in Old Shanklin, the Isle of Wight's most picturesque corner — all thatched cottages, traditional pubs and tea gardens. Shanklin Chine, the island's famous wooded gorge, is one of its oldest attractions.

Shanklin's sandy beach and esplanade are a short walk away, and the island's coastal path, Osborne House, Ventnor and the Needles make easy days out by car or bus.
