Welcome to The Coo's

A small Victorian house in Inverness with 9 en-suite rooms and a big personality. Named after the Highland coo, The Coo's Guest House is friendly, relaxed and full of Highland character — a comfortable base for the city, the River Ness and day trips to Loch Ness.
