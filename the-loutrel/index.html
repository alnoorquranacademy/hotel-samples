About The Loutrel

The Loutrel brings garden-inspired Georgian luxury to Charleston's historic French Quarter. Expect calm, contemporary comforts, a welcoming club room, a fitness studio and a rooftop terrace made for golden-hour views - all a short stroll from the city's most storied streets.
