About Thunderbird Boutique Hotel

The Thunderbird is a playful, retro-chic boutique hotel at 1215 S Las Vegas Blvd, between the Strip and Fremont Street. Rooms pair reclaimed wood beds with soft linens, rain showers and luxe toiletries, while outside the pool deck, lounge and fire pit keep the Arts District energy going.
