Welcome

Your Home in Ambleside

Run by Ann and Andrew, 3 Cambridge Villas has been welcoming Lake District travellers for many years — traditional Victorian charm, modern comforts and a personal touch throughout.

A peaceful sanctuary in the town centre

Our Victorian slate house on Church Street offers five double bedrooms and one single bedroom, all en-suite with showers, free Wi-Fi, tea and coffee making facilities and cosy bedding — designed for a restful night after a day on the fells.

Start the day with our buffet breakfast of locally sourced ingredients — hot favourites, continental choices, cereals, fresh fruit and juices — served 8:30am–9:30am, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options on request.
