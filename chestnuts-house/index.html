Welcome to Chestnuts House

Chestnuts House is a boutique Edwardian Bath-stone house offering a warm, contemporary welcome. Each of our five themed en-suite rooms is individually styled — bold stripes, soft florals and plaid throws — so no two stays feel quite the same.

Mornings begin in the bright breakfast room overlooking the garden, and the terrace and gravel garden are made for slow afternoons. With Henrietta Park on the doorstep, the house pairs a quiet residential setting with an easy stroll into the city.

By Henrietta Park, Bath

Chestnuts House sits beside Henrietta Park, one of Bath’s prettiest green spaces, in a quiet residential avenue of Bath-stone homes. From the door, an easy stroll leads to the city centre’s Georgian crescents, the Roman Baths and Bath Abbey — close to the sights, far from the bustle.
