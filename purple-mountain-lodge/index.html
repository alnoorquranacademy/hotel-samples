Our Story

Crested Butte's oldest B&B — since 1927

Originally built in 1927 as the Crested Butte Big Mine mining office, our lodge began its life as Purple Mountain Lodge in 1976 and today blends old-world charm with modern mountain amenities. Hosts Chris & Mollie Haver — Chris bought the B&B in 2003 — share their years of local knowledge so every guest makes the most of the mountains.

We're on a quiet street in the town of Crested Butte, within walking distance of the restaurants, bars and historic Elk Avenue — leave the car behind, ride our complimentary townie bikes, or take the free 3-minute town shuttle up to the Mount Crested Butte ski area. An adult-oriented property (16+).

Stay With Us

Six Plush Rooms, Each With a Private Bath

Our largest room and highest amenities, named for 13,208 ft Teocalli Mountain. The only first-floor room, with a private door to the sundeck and outdoor hot tub, large walk-in shower and two vanities.

Good To Know

Highlights

🥞 Multi-Course Gourmet Breakfast

A new gourmet breakfast each morning from Chef Chris Haver, with locally roasted Camp 4 Coffee — Italian frittatas, Belgian waffles, blue corn blueberry pancakes and mixed berry stuffed French toast.

🍺 Daily Craft Beer Tastings

Four Colorado craft beers on tap plus a rotating Beer of the Day (wine and hard cider too), Monday–Saturday — the perfect way to unwind after the mountain.

🧖 In-House Day Spa & Hot Tub

Massage in the Zen Den — the Deep Soothe full-body massage is $150 for one hour — plus a steam shower, yoga space and our outdoor hot tub under the stars.

🚲 Bikes, Ski Storage & Shuttle

Complimentary townie bikes in summer, slope-side ski storage in winter, and the free town shuttle to the ski base just a short walk away.
