Welcome

Inn of the Turquoise Bear occupies the former home of poet Witter Bynner at 342 East Buena Vista Street, Santa Fe. This rambling adobe estate, built around rooms dating to the early 1800s, welcomed guests such as Georgia O'Keeffe, Willa Cather and D.H. Lawrence. Today its nine guest rooms — most with private entrances from an acre of terraced gardens — offer kiva fireplaces, luxury linens and a full gourmet breakfast, a short walk from the Santa Fe Plaza.
