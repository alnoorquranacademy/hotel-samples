Welcome to The Woodstocker B&B

Hosted by Isabelle and Karim — exceptional Woodstock lodging with an authentic Vermont feel.

Woodstock, Vermont

One of America's prettiest villages — covered bridges, farm stands and mountain air.

Woodstock is Vermont at its most storybook: a walkable village of clapboard houses, independent shops and the Middle Covered Bridge, ringed by working farms and green hills.

Visit Billings Farm & Museum, hike Mount Tom, tour the Marsh-Billings-Rockefeller National Historical Park, and taste your way around local creameries, sugar houses and farmers' markets.
