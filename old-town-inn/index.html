A Family Inn at the Entrance to Crested Butte

Hotel amenities with the personal feel of a bed-and-breakfast — park your car and leave it there.

The Old Town Inn makes a great home base for your Crested Butte adventures, wild or civilized. You’ll find us at the entrance to town, next to Town Park, the Center for the Arts, the movie theater and the grocery store — a short stroll to the historic downtown’s shops and eateries, and to cross-country skiing, hiking and biking trailheads. The free ski-area shuttle runs right near the hotel.

Launch your day with a hearty continental breakfast and organic, locally roasted coffee — then end it with fresh-baked cookies and a soak in the outdoor hot tub. We take great care of our guests, and of our mountain home: the inn runs on 100% green-powered electricity, composts on-site and recycles comprehensively — a TripAdvisor GreenLeader and Certificate of Excellence winner.
