The Lawson Family Welcome

The Chieftain Hotel is a family-run hotel in Inverness, proudly run by the Lawson family. With 14 en-suite rooms, our own restaurant and a lively sports bar, the Chieftain is as much a gathering place for locals as a comfortable base for visitors exploring the Highlands.

Stay with us and you are within easy reach of Inverness Castle and the River Ness, with Loch Ness and the great Highland glens waiting beyond. After a day of touring, settle into the bar for the match, enjoy dinner in the restaurant, and sleep soundly in one of our comfortable en-suite rooms.

Location & Area

In Inverness, capital of the Highlands — castle, river and Loch Ness country on the doorstep.

  • Inverness Castle & River Ness — The castle viewpoint and the riverside walks of the River Ness are the city's signature sights.
  • Loch Ness — The famous loch and Urquhart Castle make an unforgettable day trip from the hotel.
  • Highland day touring — Culloden Battlefield, the Moray Firth dolphins and the great glens are all within touring distance.
  • City centre — Inverness's shops, restaurants, Eden Court Theatre and transport links are close at hand.
