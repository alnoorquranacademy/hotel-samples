Welcome

A Storied Past in Seattle — Since 1918

Built in 1918 as the Seattle Engineering School — where blacksmiths retrained for Henry Ford's assembly plant — our classic brick landmark in Uptown is now a newly renovated boutique hotel. Beveled glass, a grand staircase and vintage telephones meet spacious modern rooms, and the Tin Lizzie Lounge pours prohibition-style cocktails downstairs.
