About Patterson Inn

Patterson Inn is a stately National Landmark mansion at 420 E 11th Ave, Denver, home to nine individually themed suites - from Antoinette to DaVinci. Grand halls, a welcoming lounge and richly detailed rooms make every stay feel like a small, elegant escape into another era.
