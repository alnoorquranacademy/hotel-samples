Welcome

Take Centre Stage in New Orleans

The Pelham's guestrooms are tailor-made for rest — and a little drama. Open the curtains of a street-facing Victorian window to the bustle of Common and Magazine Streets, then sink into theatre-inspired furnishings, designer amenities and marble-lined bathrooms beneath 18-foot ceilings.
