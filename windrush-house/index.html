Welcome

Owner-run, in the traditional way

Windrush House is a classical, contemporary bed and breakfast in Broadway, run personally by its owners, Kate and Robin.

Each of the five bedrooms is named after a much-loved Cotswold village, and the house looks onto a landscaped garden — a peaceful base for walking, exploring Broadway’s honey-stone high street, or simply unwinding.

It is the kind of place where the details are looked after by the people who own them: home baking, a comfortable lounge, and traditional values throughout.
