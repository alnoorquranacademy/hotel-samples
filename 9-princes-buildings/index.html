Welcome to 9 Princes Buildings

9 Princes Buildings is a family-owned bed & breakfast in a classic Georgian townhouse in Clifton, Bristol — our tagline says it all: Clifton's hidden secret. Expect elegant period rooms, a warm homestay welcome and wonderful views towards the Avon Gorge and the Clifton Suspension Bridge.

Location & the area

9 Princes Buildings is in Clifton, Bristol's elegant Georgian quarter, overlooking the Avon Gorge. Walk to the Clifton Suspension Bridge, browse Clifton Village's boutiques and cafés, and visit the Downs and Bristol Zoo Gardens nearby.
