Welcome

A 1787 colonial landmark, reimagined as a boutique hotel

Built in 1787 and now a National Historic Landmark, the Morris House Hotel is a boutique colonial hotel and event venue made of three beautiful historic buildings, just off Washington Square Park and a few blocks from Independence Hall. Contemporary sophistication that respects its 18th-century heritage - with a 24-hour concierge, complimentary continental breakfast and afternoon refreshments.

  • Complimentary continental breakfast
  • Afternoon refreshments
  • 24-hour concierge service
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Courtyard garden & terrace
  • Library & historic parlours
  • Weddings & events for up to 200
  • Luxury bedding & fine finishing touches
