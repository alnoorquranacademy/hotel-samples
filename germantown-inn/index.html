Welcome

Welcome to the Germantown Inn

Step into a beautifully appointed 1865 Federal-style home, reimagined as a ten-suite boutique hotel in historic Germantown. Each suite is uniquely designed and named for a notable figure from American history, blending 19th-century architecture with contemporary luxury, fine linens and thoughtful modern touches.

Rooms & Suites
