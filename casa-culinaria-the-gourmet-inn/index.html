Welcome

Casa Culinaria - The Gourmet Inn is set within three distinctive historic homes at 617 Don Gaspar Avenue, Santa Fe. No two rooms are alike: spacious interiors, curated furnishings, fine linens, private entrances and garden views, with many rooms opening onto patios and quiet courtyards. At the heart of the inn is its gourmet brunch, prepared each morning from seasonal ingredients.
