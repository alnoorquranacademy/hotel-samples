Welcome

Originally built in 1873 as a private home, the Hamilton-Turner Inn stands on historic Lafayette Square at 330 Abercorn Street, Savannah. Its French Empire architecture and original interior elements carry you back 150 years. Days begin with a chef-prepared Southern breakfast; evenings bring wine and hors d'oeuvres at the Innkeeper's reception, and port and cookies by the fireplace in the parlor.
