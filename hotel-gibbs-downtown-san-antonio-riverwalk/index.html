Welcome

San Antonio’s first high-rise, thoughtfully restored

Originally built in 1909 as San Antonio’s first high-rise, Hotel Gibbs preserves its historic roots - warm wood floors, marble halls and Alamo heritage - while offering a stay designed for today. It stands on the grounds of the original Alamo compound, just steps from the Alamo itself and 100 feet from the River Walk entrance.

  • Daily hot breakfast buffet
  • Complimentary FlorVino wine tasting (21+)
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • 24-hour fitness center
  • 1909 Bar and Bistro on site
  • Pet friendly
  • Historic marble lobby
  • Steps from the Alamo & River Walk
