A Cliffside Inn Above Frenchman Bay

Welcoming guests to Mount Desert Island for over 60 years, on a ledge above the water where the mountains meet the sea.

The Inn at Bay Ledge has been welcoming visitors to Mount Desert Island and the Acadia region for over 60 years. Our three buildings — the Main Inn with 7 guest rooms, the Cottages at Sunset Pines with 3 guest rooms, and the private Summer Cottage — sit above an 80-foot cliff on Frenchman Bay, just three minutes from the nearest Acadia National Park entrance, yet away from the crowds of downtown Bar Harbor.

Wake to sunrise views above our private beach, take the stairs down to hunt for sea glass, then return for a house-made breakfast, afternoon tea and treats, a soak in the hot tubs or a session in the sauna.

Your hosts — Sarah & Dax Wandling
Innkeepers since October 2022, Sarah and Dax (with their bernedoodle, Stanley) fulfilled a lifelong dream of running a bed and breakfast — and can’t wait to meet you.

Breakfast & Afternoon Treats

Come for the view, stay for the breakfast. Our prix fixe breakfast is served daily from 8:00–9:30 am in the sun room or on the back deck — gourmet quiches, homemade pastries and scratch-made cinnamon rolls, with locally roasted coffee ready by 7:00. Each afternoon, tea is served with homemade treats, from chocolate chip cookies to lemon-rosemary shortbread.
