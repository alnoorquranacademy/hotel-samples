Welcome to The Kennard

Built in 1794, The Kennard is a Georgian town house hotel of charm and character. Inside, expect boutique, timeless decor: toile wallpapers, gilt mirrors, deep armchairs and a sweeping staircase beneath a chandelier.

Days start with a continental breakfast in the elegant dining room — homemade granola, fruit, pastries and proper coffee — and the honesty bar is open when you return. Every one of our twelve rooms is different; choose yours below.

Exploring Bath

The Kennard stands on one of Bath’s classic Georgian crescents, within walking distance of the Roman Baths, Bath Abbey, No. 1 Royal Crescent and the Thermae Bath Spa. The American Museum, the Holburne Museum and the Bath Skyline walk are all nearby — the whole city is a stroll from our door.
