A Home From Home in the West End

Argyll Guest House is a friendly, budget-conscious guest house in Glasgow's much-loved West End, close to Kelvingrove Park and the Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum. With 18 rooms, it is big enough to welcome groups and families, yet small enough to keep the personal, homely feel our guests return for.

Inside you will find comfortable rooms with rustic, tartan touches, and in the morning a hearty Scottish breakfast sets you up for a day of exploring the city. Whether you are visiting the university, attending an event, or simply discovering Glasgow, you will find a warm welcome here.

Location & Area

In the heart of Glasgow's West End — the city's café, gallery and park quarter.

  • Kelvingrove Park & Museum — The West End's beloved park and Glasgow's famous free museum and art gallery are right in the neighbourhood.
  • University of Glasgow — The historic university, with its landmark tower and cloisters, is a short walk away.
  • Byres Road & Ashton Lane — Independent shops, cafés, bars and restaurants along the West End's main streets.
  • The River Clyde & City Centre — Easy transport links carry you to the city centre, the SEC and Glasgow's venues.
