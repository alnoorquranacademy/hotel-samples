Welcome

Turn-of-the-century charm, one block from the river

The Fulton House began life in 1880 and was moved to its present site in the 1930s - today this beautifully restored house in historic John’s Landing welcomes travellers with four charming rooms, no two alike. Enjoy the scenic horizon from the rooftop perch, imagine 1880s Fulton commerce from the second-floor porch, or relax in the tranquil garden. Oh - if this house could talk.

  • Complimentary homemade breakfast
  • Private bathroom in every room
  • Free high-speed Wi-Fi
  • Tranquil garden & patio
  • Rooftop perch with river views
  • Second-floor porch
  • Free parking
  • One block from Willamette Park
