Welcome

Beach Hut Bed and Breakfast sits at 3761 Riviera Drive in Crown Point, Pacific Beach, inside the 4,200-acre Mission Bay Park. Guests enjoy private exterior entrances, reserved on-site parking, high-speed WiFi, a saline pool with solar heating, and a marble fire table on a professionally landscaped patio. Breakfast items are selected by each guest in advance and placed in the room, so mornings begin exactly the way you like them.

Rooms & Rates

Garden Room

A serene queen room in a separate building, overlooking the garden flowers, pool and fountain. Private entrance, kitchen nook, travertine and granite bathroom, and a cosy faux fireplace.

Published tariff: $249-$285 per night · 2-night minimum

Tuscan Room

Our premium room at the front of the main house, in warm Tuscan colours with vineyard accents. Queen bed, large bay window with a peek of Sail Bay, brick faux fireplace and a stocked breakfast kitchen.

Published tariff: $249-$285 per night · 2-night minimum

Rates are as published by the inn: $249-$285 per night with a 2-night minimum. Reserved parking, WiFi, beach chairs, umbrella and towels are included.
