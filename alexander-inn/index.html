About Capitol Hill Mansion Bed & Breakfast Inn

Capitol Hill Mansion is an 1891 ruby sandstone landmark in Denver's Capitol Hill, offering eight rooms and suites named for Colorado wildflowers. Days begin with a garden brunch mood and end in rooms full of period charm, fireplaces and thoughtful modern comforts.
