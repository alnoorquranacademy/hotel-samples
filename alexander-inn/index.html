Welcome

Ocean-liner Art Deco, Philadelphia warmth

Behind the red doors at 301 South 12th Street, the Alexander Inn pairs 1930s ocean-liner Art Deco design with the ease of a neighbourhood inn. Forty-eight designer rooms, a complimentary continental breakfast, and an evening lobby social with wine and snacks - all in the heart of Center City, one block from the Broad Street subway line.

  • Complimentary continental breakfast
  • Evening lobby social - wine & snacks, 5:00-7:30pm
  • Pet friendly - pets stay free
  • Free WiFi throughout
  • 24-hour front desk
  • Discounted parking nearby at Parkway Garage
  • Bay-window seats & city views in Deluxe rooms
  • One block from Broad Street subway
