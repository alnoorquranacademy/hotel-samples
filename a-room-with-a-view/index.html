Welcome to A Room With A View

A Room With A View is a boutique bed & breakfast on Brighton seafront, in a Grade II listed Georgian building, rated 4.9 on Google. Its nine rooms range from a cosy single to a deluxe king — and true to our name, the sea views from our rooms and rooftop balcony are unforgettable.
