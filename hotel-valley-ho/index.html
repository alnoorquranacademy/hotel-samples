Welcome

A Jail You Will Never Want to Leave

The Inn at the Old Jail is a quirky, playful boutique inn inside Tremé's historic 1902 jail building, listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Behind the original doors you will find soaring brick cell-block rooms, comfortable beds, new bathrooms and a rooftop terrace — a stay that could only happen in New Orleans.
