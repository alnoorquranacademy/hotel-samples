Welcome

Scottsdale, Done Differently — Since 1956

An icon of mid-century modern design in the heart of Old Town, Hotel Valley Ho pairs colourful retro architecture with lush resort grounds. Dine at ZuZu, lounge under the palms at the OH Pool or the serene Ohasis Pool, unwind at VH Spa, and keep moving in the 24/7 fitness centre.
