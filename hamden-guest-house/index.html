Welcome to Hamden Guest House

Hamden Guest House is a welcoming family-run B&B in Cambridge, ideal for visitors to the university city, business travellers and families alike. Guests rate us 5.0 on Google. Every room rate includes a Continental breakfast, and our garden is a peaceful spot to unwind after a day in the city.

Location & the area

Hamden Guest House is in Cambridge, the famous university city on the River Cam. Visit the historic colleges, see King's College Chapel, punt along the Backs past the Mathematical Bridge, and enjoy the city's museums, shops and cafés — all within easy reach of the house.
