Welcome to Beachcomber

A family-run B&B, hosted by Sharon & Andrew

Sharon & Andrew Dominey took over the Beachcomber in November 2019 and have lovingly renovated it since — upgrading the beds, mattresses and bedding, fitting a new kitchen, and giving the outside of the building a fresh, modern look.

You'll find us at 12 Deer Park, Greenhill Road, in a prominent spot in the picturesque town of Tenby, Pembrokeshire — one of the few B&Bs in Tenby to offer parking facilities to guests. During your stay, Sharon & Andrew are always on hand to make sure you have a wonderful stay.

Highlights

🍳 Locally Sourced Breakfast

Cooked breakfast with pork sausage from a local award-winning butcher and free-range eggs — plus our much-loved Eggs Benedict, cereals, fresh fruit and natural yoghurt. Served 8:30–9:30am; dietary requirements catered for with notice.

🅿️ Private On-Site Parking

One of the few B&Bs in Tenby offering parking for guests — and it's free when you book direct by phone.

🏖️ Minutes From the Beach

Just a few minutes' walk from Tenby town centre and its three Blue Flag beaches, in the Pembrokeshire Coast National Park.

Tenby train station is a 10-minute walk away, with links to Swansea, Bristol and beyond.
