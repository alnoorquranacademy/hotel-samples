Welcome to Benson House

Benson House pairs monochrome minimalism with muted classical elegance. Expect calm, carefully kept rooms: natural fibres, Egyptian cotton, soft lamplight and thoughtful details from the embroidered towelling to the striped armchairs.

All five rooms are en-suite, and breakfast — served in our pretty dining room — is included in every rate. It is a quiet, personal kind of luxury, moments from the life of the city.
