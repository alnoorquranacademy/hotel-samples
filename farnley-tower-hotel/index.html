Welcome

Farnley Tower is a traditional Victorian mansion guesthouse in Durham, home to 17 en-suite rooms. From the house, the city's two great landmarks — Durham Castle and Durham Cathedral, together a UNESCO World Heritage Site — rise above the River Wear, and the historic city centre is an easy stroll away. Expect a warm, personal welcome and rooms for every kind of stay, from solo trips to family visits and our special Deluxe Attic Suite.

A Traditional Victorian Welcome

Behind the mansion's Victorian façade you'll find period features, comfortable lounges and the calm of a family-run guesthouse — the perfect counterpoint to a busy day of sightseeing in the city.

Location & Area

Durham — Castle, Cathedral & the River Wear

Farnley Tower Hotel is in Durham, the historic city wrapped around a loop of the River Wear. Walk down to the riverbanks for rowing boats and woodland paths, climb to Palace Green between the Castle and Cathedral, and explore the cobbled streets, cafés and shops of the old city — all within easy reach of the house.
