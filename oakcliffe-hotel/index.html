Welcome to Oakcliffe Hotel

Tucked into a handsome late-Victorian house, Oakcliffe has been welcoming guests to Exeter since 1961. As a small family-run guest house, every stay is personal: a warm greeting, a comfortable room and local advice from people who know and love this city.

Our rooms are offered on a room-only basis, giving you the freedom to start the day your way — exploring Exeter's cafés, the historic Cathedral Green and the Quayside at your own pace. Guests rate us 5.0 on Google, and we work hard to earn that welcome every single day.

Exploring Exeter

Exeter is one of Devon's great historic cities. The magnificent Cathedral and its green are the city's beating heart, while the old Quayside on the River Exe offers waterside walks, cafés and independent shops. From Oakcliffe you are well placed for the city centre, the university and onward journeys across Devon — to Dartmoor, the Exe estuary and the south coast.
