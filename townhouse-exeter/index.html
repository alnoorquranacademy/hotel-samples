Welcome to Townhouse Exeter

Townhouse Exeter blends contemporary style with the character of a period townhouse. Behind the front door you will find nine stylish en-suite rooms, each individually finished — crisp duvets, thoughtful design touches and space to truly unwind.

Our signature is simple: duvets, dishes and a proper guesthouse welcome. Breakfast is served in-room, so your morning can start slowly, exactly as it should — before a day exploring Exeter's cathedral quarter, independent shops and historic quayside.

Exploring Exeter

Stay in the heart of historic Exeter. Cathedral Green and the great west front of Exeter Cathedral are the city's postcard view, the Cathedral Close is lined with handsome period buildings, and the Quayside offers riverside dining and walks along the Exe. Townhouse Exeter puts the best of the city within easy reach.
