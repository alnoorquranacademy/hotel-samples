Welcome to Bed and Breakfast Fredericksburg Texas

Not a typical hotel — a genuine Hill Country ranch experience that becomes part of the trip itself.

Set on 31 gated acres, our property pairs modern, beautifully appointed accommodation with a working-ranch atmosphere you won't find at any hotel. Longhorns, blackbuck, goats and peacocks roam the grounds — wildlife is quite literally part of the view.

Fredericksburg's famous wineries, restaurants, shopping and attractions are all within easy reach, so you get the serenity of the ranch with the town right there when you want it.

Fredericksburg & Texas Wine Country

German heritage, 50+ wineries and the best of the Hill Country — minutes away.

Fredericksburg is the heart of Texas Wine Country, with dozens of wineries and tasting rooms along Highway 290, plus a Main Street full of German bakeries, boutiques and live music.

Climb Enchanted Rock, visit the National Museum of the Pacific War, and time a trip for peach season or one of the town's many festivals — then retreat to the quiet of the ranch.
