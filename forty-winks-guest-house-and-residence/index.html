Welcome

Forty Winks Guest House & Residence is a luxury boutique Victorian townhouse in Durham, rated 5.0 on Google by its guests. The house offers five luxury suites and four individually styled rooms, each one designed with its own personality — and the city's magnificent cathedral, whose skyline you see above, is the backdrop to your stay. This is Durham at its most elegant: calm, beautiful and quietly luxurious.

The Roll-Top Bath Ritual

The Gala Super-King Suite's roll-top bath is made for slowing down — run it deep, pour something lovely, and let the day dissolve. It's the detail our guests talk about long after they've gone home.

Location & Area

Durham & Its Cathedral Skyline

Forty Winks stands in Durham, within sight of the cathedral that dominates one of England's most beautiful small-city skylines. Stroll to the cathedral and castle on Palace Green, drift along the River Wear, and return to the peace of the townhouse and its pretty garden at the end of the day.
