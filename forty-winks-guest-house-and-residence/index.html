Welcome

Grosvenor Place Guest House is a central Chester guest house offering four en-suite, room-only rooms in clean, coordinated modern style. With convenient self check-in, it is a simple, comfortable base for exploring Chester's famous Rows, city walls and cathedral.

Location & Exploring Chester

From Grosvenor Place you are in the heart of Chester: shop in the unique two-tier Rows, walk the complete circuit of the Roman city walls, visit Chester Cathedral, and stroll down to the River Dee and the Old Dee Bridge — all within easy walking distance.
