Welcome to Austins Guesthouse

From the moment you walk in, you will feel the Austins difference: super-soft linen, spotless rooms and smart self check-in — your room assignment and pin code arrive by email and text on the day, so you can let yourself in whenever you arrive.

Choose a deluxe double, our apartment-style family suite sleeping five, or a simple single. Free Wi-Fi runs throughout, every room has tea and coffee making facilities, and late check-out is available when you need a slower morning.

Quick answers

How does self check-in work?
On the day of check-in we email and text your room assignment and pin code — the code opens the front door and your room, so you can arrive when it suits you.

Is Wi-Fi included?
Yes — free Wi-Fi is available in every room, and each room has tea and coffee making facilities.

Can I check out late?
We offer late check-outs if you need more time on your departure day — just ask when you book.

Can I book direct?
Yes — direct is best. Call 029 2105 1510 or email austinsguesthousecardiff@gmail.com and we will confirm availability and rates personally.
