Welcome

Welcome to the Inn at Palm Springs

All of our rooms have tile or hardwood floors and open to fresh air, and most have pool, mountain or garden views. We are centrally located, close to the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway — a friendly, inclusive spot for everyone, dogs very much included.
