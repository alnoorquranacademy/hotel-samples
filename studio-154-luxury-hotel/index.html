Welcome

A Sanctuary Against Scottsdale's Historic Downtown

Mission-inspired and lushly planted, The Scott blends desert charm with the spirit of faraway places. Across 204 guest rooms and suites you will find a lagoon pool and a sand-bottom pool with cabanas, Caribbean-inspired dining at The Canal Club, and deep calm at La Vidorra Spa.
