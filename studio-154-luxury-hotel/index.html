Welcome

Welcome to Studio 154

In the heart of downtown Nashville, Studio 154 blends modern luxury with urban convenience. Broadway's live music, the Ryman Auditorium, Bridgestone Arena and the Riverfront are all within walking distance, and our rooftop Skydeck looks out over the Cumberland River and the skyline.
