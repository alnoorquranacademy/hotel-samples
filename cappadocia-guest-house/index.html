Welcome to Cappadocia Guest House

Cappadocia Guest House is a boutique hideaway in the North Laine, Brighton’s most creative quarter. Newly refurbished, our rooms mix comfort with character: exposed brick chimney breasts, wooden wardrobes, warm throws and ornate cushions.

Look up and you will spot our signature touch — hand-made Turkish mosaic lamps, a nod to the Cappadocia that gave us our name. Every room is en-suite, and the seafront, the Lanes and the station are all an easy stroll away.
