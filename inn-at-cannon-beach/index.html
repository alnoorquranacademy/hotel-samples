Welcome to Inn at Cannon Beach

Restore your joy as a guest of the Inn at Cannon Beach — a five-to-seven-minute walk puts you on the sand.

The Inn sits in the peaceful Tolovana Park neighborhood at the south end of Cannon Beach, just one short residential block from the ocean. Haystack Rock, Oregon's iconic 235-foot sea stack, is about a 15-minute stroll north along the beach.

Our rooms are arranged around a central courtyard with a pond and lush plantings. Spend an afternoon in the sun in an Adirondack chair, then build a bonfire on the beach with a basket of s'more makings and a thermos of cocoa.

Cannon Beach & Haystack Rock

Oregon's most beloved beach town — galleries, cafés and a legendary sea stack.

Cannon Beach pairs wild Pacific scenery with a village full of galleries, cafés and boutiques. The Inn is in Tolovana Park, the quiet south end of town — peaceful, but an easy walk or drive to everything.

Explore the tide pools at Haystack Rock at low tide, hike the trails of Ecola State Park, and watch the sun set behind the sea stacks with a bonfire on the sand.
