A Warm Family Welcome, 25 Years Strong

The Franklin is a family-run guesthouse in Harrogate, the celebrated North Yorkshire spa town. For over 25 years our family has welcomed guests the way we would welcome friends — with comfortable en-suite rooms, a relaxed atmosphere and genuine Yorkshire hospitality.

Harrogate's famous gardens, historic baths and tea rooms are all part of the experience here. Whether you are visiting for a conference, a wedding, a show at the Royal Hall, or a gentle few days among the flowers of Valley Gardens, you will be looked after from the moment you arrive.

Location & Area

In the heart of Harrogate — gardens, baths, tea rooms and theatres within easy reach.

  • Valley Gardens — Harrogate's award-winning gardens, with the Sun Pavilion and seasonal flower displays, are a short stroll away.
  • Royal Pump Room & Turkish Baths — Discover the spa heritage that made Harrogate famous — the museum and the beautifully restored Turkish Baths.
  • Tea rooms & Montpellier Quarter — Classic Yorkshire tea rooms, independent shops and galleries around the town centre.
  • The Stray — Two hundred acres of open parkland wrapping around the town — perfect for a morning walk.
