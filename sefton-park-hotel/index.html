Welcome

Victorian heritage, boutique comfort

Sefton Park Hotel is a striking Victorian villa on Aigburth Drive, looking out over the green expanse of Sefton Park.

Inside you will find 16 individually styled en-suite rooms, our bar and bistro, and a garden terrace for sunny afternoons. The separate Coach House sleeps six, making it a favourite for groups and wedding parties.

The house is also celebrated for its Stuart Sutcliffe and Beatles memorabilia — a must-see for fans exploring Liverpool’s musical story.
