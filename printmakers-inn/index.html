Welcome

A Printmaker's Legacy in the Historic District

Printmaker's Inn pairs two remarkable Savannah houses: the Nichols House (1884), a Victorian Italianate built for printer William Nichols — head printer's son in Civil-War-era Savannah — and the Hills-Galloway House (c. 1693), carefully relocated from Connecticut and restored beam by beam. Expect era antiques, heart-pine floors, original ceiling medallions and modern comforts throughout.
